# Code of conduct

rea-captcha welcomes people who contribute code, tests, documentation, bug reports, and careful questions. Treat other participants with respect, assume good faith, and keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear, constructive, and patient.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Protect private information. Never post credentials, cookies, browser profiles, challenge tokens, or captured private-page evidence.
- Discuss CAPTCHA behavior only in owned, authorized, or provider-documented test environments.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope, safety boundaries, and moderation.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of private information, and repeated disruptive promotion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [masaka-ai-sup@outlook.com](mailto:masaka-ai-sup@outlook.com). Include links or screenshots when safe, but redact credentials and private browser data. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be reviewed as privately and consistently as practical.

This policy applies in the repository and in project spaces where someone is representing rea-captcha or MASAKA AI.
